# Empirical support provenance

`audit_driver.py` is the exact external audit executed against the closed primary store. `replication_driver.py` is the exact fresh-process comparison driver; neither modifies `poc/`, the frozen recipe, or primary producer artifacts. Raw paths are explicit invocation arguments. The audit reads all resource/error/command records and losslessly counts decoded gzip bytes. The replica regenerates session/evaluation, selected tensors, first/last prediction and decision outputs, and designated three-resolution truth.

The published projection includes these transparent post-audit additions: requested-development total is the sum of `projected_development_total_wall_s` across top-level stage rows. Incremental wall is that total minus the sum of `pilot_wall_s`, plus the pilot timing wall (development requires its own complete timing request). Cumulative campaign wall is requested-development total plus pilot timing wall. Additional fitting wall is the development fit row minus pilot fit row. Cumulative retained artifact bytes are requested-development artifact bytes plus pilot timing producer bytes. Closed CLI command records are copied from `*.execution.json`; UTC-minus-monotonic duration is calendar elapsed minus the declared command monotonic elapsed. These additions never change scientific cells, thresholds, gate or raw records. Timing scales by 210/24 coordinates; it is an approximate prospective workload estimate. New adaptive truth/report/verification overhead can add cost.

The final report records hashes of these exact support bytes and generated exports. Primary report/verify already validated full raw coverage; the replica store intentionally has only the three prescribed fits and does not claim complete pilot or development coverage.

For a public-checkout fresh numerical replay, first use the unchanged CLI to `prepare --matrix pilot --output-root NEW_ROOT`, `collect --matrix pilot --output-root NEW_ROOT --session 0`, then `train --matrix pilot --output-root NEW_ROOT --session 0 --model MODEL --budget 30 --initialization 0` in separate serial processes for `affine_rk4`, `node_actuator_aware`, `physics_residual`. Run `.venv/bin/python reports/short_calibration_pilot/replication_driver.py PRIMARY_ROOT NEW_ROOT` from the repository. The driver requires the completed primary raw store. Archived orchestration/stage scripts preserve the exact historical local invocation; fresh roots and explicit arguments avoid returning cached primary jobs.
