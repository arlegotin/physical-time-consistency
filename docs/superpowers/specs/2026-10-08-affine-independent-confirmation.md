# Fresh-data confirmation of the affine-flow result

Protocol: PTCO-AFF-0.2. This is a targeted continuation of Task 3 in the affine-flow refinement plan, selected from PTCO-AFF-0.1 development. It confirms a new method version; it does not change PTCO-1.0's original verdict or imply a universal robot-model advantage.

1. **Purpose.** Test the development finding that affine-flow improves fine-time prediction on the known-lag two-link robot while using less batched computation than the strongest tested learned predictor, the mechanics-aware NODE. Quantify whether that matters to the unchanged controller. All positive and negative findings remain visible. The original power head is a different implementation; its earlier confirmed MLP wins remain separate.
2. **Entry.** Both new nominal fits and the complete prediction measurement are valid; the lagged 5 ms pilot has lower q/v error and lower batch128 latency than structured NODE. The 80-episode development control batch must finish before independent computation begins. Existing numerical qualifications, local CPU authorization and exact pinned environment apply. No paid service, new robot, publication or architecture search is authorized.
3. **Implementation.** Add version-specific corpus/measurement orchestration in new modules. Do not change any frozen model, trainer, planner or old producer. Use the same TrainingSession, loss, optimizer, normalization calculation and nominal convergence rule for AffineFlow and node_structured. Preserve all complete and interrupted attempts, exact optimizer/RNG resumption and hash-bound inputs.
4. **Design.** Only lagged actuation, tau=80 ms. Fresh A corpora have 1,024 train and 256 validation trajectories, 32 held commands per trajectory, with exactly the original initial-state/command/20–60 ms duration distributions. SeedSequence entropy is [20261008,2,role_code,1,trajectory_id,channel], role codes train=0, validation=1, confirmation=2, channels initial=0, commands=1, durations=2, probe command=3. Both models receive the same fully observed q/v/w, commands, known tau and endpoint labels, comparable parameter counts (18,182 versus 17,922) and the same nominal optimizer opportunity. Their structural priors differ and are disclosed.

   Each model uses A/shared one-plus-four-step loss, AdamW1e-3, effective batch256, 100-epoch blocks and nominal-only selection. The existing stabilized/half-constant-velocity qualification and prespecified3e-4 repair apply equally. Continue until nominal convergence; report actual exposure rather than force equal training time or silently weaken a comparator.    Primary per-training-seed prediction statistics are the equally weighted cold/warm mean two-joint q and v error norms at5 ms. 8. **Decision rules.** The primary combined claim requires positive paired improvements in all three prespecified quantities: q5 ms error, v5 ms error and batch1285 ms latency. Use paired training-seed clusters, Student-t one-sided bounds and a20,000-resample common paired cluster bootstrap; take the more conservative bound and expand for numerical error. At look l spend alpha_l=0.05/2^l. This is an intersection-union claim, with no convenient selection among components. Zero variance in an empirical unbounded contrast triggers diagnosis/additional independent evidence, not automatic certainty. Report ratios and exact units; a zero baseline denominator cannot count as a win. There is no50% or other minimum improvement floor. An incomplete, invalid or unresolved primary claim cannot be called confirmed.

This is a reporting precision target, not a required improvement or a universal robot-utility standard. Predictive/speed unresolved conclusions similarly require bounded improvement widths<=0.20/0.10. A resolved sign needs no artificial effect floor.
9. **Transitions.** Valid primary gain plus measured control/compute tradeoffs and passed reproduction -> final shareable result. Wider/unstable intervals -> record an explicit precision request before new outcomes, add at least five ascending independent seeds, or double paired tasks if within-seed episode uncertainty accounts for>70% of total. Retain old samples and alpha looks. Do not stop at a round seed count; stop at resolved signs/declared precision or an exact real resource prerequisite. A near-zero unresolved control effect can finish as an honestly bounded finding once the specified precision is attained. Invalid evidence -> minimized defect/repair, never hypothesis rejection.
