A zero validation denominator passes only exact equality. Corrupt tensors/identity/provenance are invalid; a valid tolerance mismatch requires reproduction diagnosis.

It preserves every original task, planner, clock and random stream, replacing the model manifest/checkpoint and phase. After read-only primary raw closure, the comparison substitutes the repeated costs at seeds 0 and 4, leaves the other three seed clusters unchanged and recomputes the existing eight criterion decisions at their original look and accepted numerical envelope. It records zero new clusters and zero alpha spending. Tolerance, success/fallback or criterion mismatches require reproduction diagnosis; this diagnostic creates no new scientific verdict.

The retraining receipt covers weights and nominal validation only. Reproduced control remains a separate required measurement: use all twenty original paired tasks for seeds 0 and 4, every model/plant and both clocks (480 episodes), replacing only weights and phase. Independently reconcile realized costs, require relative control-cost reproduction within `1e-3`, and check that substituting these repeated seed costs preserves the eight criterion decisions at their existing look. This is a reproducibility diagnostic, not new independent clusters or a fresh alpha budget. Do not mark criterion stability, full C06 or completion from the twelve-fit receipt. `--dry-run` creates no context, checkpoint, replay policy or scientific result.
